# Improve the wardrobe setup layout

## What will change

- Keep Layerly’s current colors, typography, icons, and two-column card grid.
- Give every clothing card the same visual structure: icon, item name, warmth tag, then a short example.
- Align names and warmth tags consistently so longer labels wrap without pushing tags into awkward positions.
- Remove the long “What counts?” paragraphs from individual cards; place one compact guidance panel beneath the grid for the choices that need clarification.
- Reduce excess empty space in simple cards while keeping every choice comfortably tappable.
- Preserve selection states, warmth-tag colors, progress, and existing wardrobe behavior.

## Responsive layout

- Keep two columns on iPhone while allowing card text to wrap cleanly.
- Use stable card dimensions and spacing so rows feel even rather than alternating between sparse and crowded.
- Ensure the guidance panel and bottom controls remain readable above the iPhone safe area.

## Verification

- Check the Mid layers step at an iPhone-sized viewport.
- Confirm long names, warmth tags, and descriptions do not overlap or clip.
